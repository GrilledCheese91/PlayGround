HOW TO PLAY

Lead your hero: right-click a location to move, or right-click an enemy to hunt them down. On touchscreens, tap to move; tap an enemy to attack.

Build your force: footmen march from each base to the center every 5 seconds. Fight for gold, upgrade your army, and help your guard dominate the crossroads.
